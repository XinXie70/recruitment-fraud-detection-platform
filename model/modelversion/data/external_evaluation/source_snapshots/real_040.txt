Please visit our website: www.turnerandtownsend.com We are seeking an experienced Senior Scheduler / Planner to support major infrastructure programs. You will play a key role in schedule development, analysis, and reporting across all project phases. Schedule Coordination : Collaborate with internal teams and external stakeholders to gather and align schedule inputs across planning, pre-construction, construction, and commissioning. Project Integration : Work closely with technical advisors, client experts, contractors, and subcontractors to ensure seamless schedule integration. Progress Monitoring : Track, analyse, and report on schedule performance, achievements, and forecasts. Schedule Analysis : Conduct quality checks, critical path analysis, DCMA assessments, and schedule comparisons. Risk & Performance Analysis : Perform schedule risk assessments, Earned Value Management (EVM), and Schedule Performance Index (SPI) monitoring. Forensic & Delay Analysis : Lead forensic reviews and delay analysis to support claims and project reviews. Dashboard Reporting : Present complex schedule data in clear, visual formats using dashboards and summary reports. We’re looking for a driven and collaborative professional with a strong technical foundation and excellent communication skills. Industry Experience : Proven background as a Senior Scheduler, Planner, or Schedule Manager in infrastructure (rail, utilities, aviation, civil, ports, transport or defence). Software Proficiency : Advanced Primavera P6 skills essential; experience with Safran, Acumen Fuse, or Primavera Risk is highly desirable. Data Visualisation : Power BI experience is an advantage for reporting and dashboard creation. Client-Side Experience : Exposure to both consultancy and contractor environments is beneficial. Security Clearance : Eligibility to apply for Baseline Defence Clearance is advantageous.
