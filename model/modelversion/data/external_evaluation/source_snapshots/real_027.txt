About the Role This is a technical program manager role at its heart, and a chance to own a problem that really matters: making Canva feel like it was built for you, whatever language you think in. You'll be the person who brings together every team working on Canva's GenAI localisation goal - product, research, country teams, evaluation specialists and external partners - and turns a sprawling, ambiguous challenge into a clear plan everyone can get behind. About 70-80% of your time is program leadership and operations: setting the direction, rallying people, and building the systems that let us measure and improve localisation quality at scale. The other 20-30% is hands-on enough that you can spot patterns in the data and speak credibly with the technical teams. You don't need to be a localisation expert or have trained models yourself - you need to be brilliant at bringing order to complexity and getting cross-functional teams moving in the same direction. This is a 12 month fixed term contract. What you’ll do Lead the program to make Canva's GenAI localisation goal real - holding the plan, the timeline and the people together Support with desiging evaluation operations at scale, including human review pipelines in Label Studio across many languages and building the foundations for automated evals Manage the contingent labeller workforce and external vendors who power evaluation, including capacity planning and quality standards Partner with the GenAI evaluation teams to define what "good" looks like for localisation quality Build lightweight dashboards and tooling to track quality and surface issues - comfortable vibe-coding your way to a working view Spot patterns in localisation quality data and translate them into recommendations the technical teams can act on Communicate progress, risks and decisions clearly to stakeholders from labellers all the way up to leadership What we're looking for You're a program or operations leader who thrives in ambiguity and loves bringing order to chaos You've run complex cross-functional programs where success depended on rallying people you don't manage You're comfortable enough with data and AI concepts - things like LLM evaluation, RAG, BLEU or COMET - to hold your own with technical teams, even if you've never trained a model You can manage a workforce of reviewers and vendors without losing sight of quality You can move fluidly between a labeller's day-to-day and a leadership update without missing a beat You've delivered AI or transformation programs from 0-to-1 at enterprise scale - turning a blank page into a shipped, repeatable system Backgrounds that tend to fit: technical program management, AI program or transformation roles, principal program management, or product operations Speaking multiple languages is a huge bonus Other stuff to know We make hiring decisions based on your experience, skills and passion, as well as how you can enhance Canva and our culture.
