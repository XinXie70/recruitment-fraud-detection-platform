A220 First Officer – National Jet Systems. Employer: Qantas Group. Location: Sydney, New South Wales, Australia. Employment type: Full-time. An exciting time to join the team as we continue to grow our Airbus A220 fleet. Opportunities in Brisbane, Melbourne, and Sydney. As we continue to increase our fleet of A220’s, it is an exciting time to join National Jet Systems. The A220 offers substantial opportunities for our Flight Crew into the future and introduces leading industry best-practice in the way we train and promote our crews. National Jet Systems are currently accepting applications for First Officers on the A220 fleet. Opportunities will be across our Melbourne, Sydney & Brisbane bases. Minimum Experience Required: Minimum 500 hours flight time in a fixed wing aircraft and at least one of the following minimum requirements: 200 hours in command of a multi engine aircraft (excluding PICUS). 200 hours in command of a turbine powered fixed wing aircraft; or 200 hours on aircraft certified for operation by a crew of two pilots (multi-crew aircraft) Essential Qualifications CASA ATPL (A) or CASA CPL(A) with passes in all Australian ATPL theory subjects. For candidates holding a CPL, MCC course completion certificate or an equivalent qualification Multi Engine Aeroplane Class Rating Multi Engine Aeroplane Instrument Rating with IAP 2D and IAP 3D CASA Class 1 Medical Certificate English Language Proficiency Level 6; and The ability to hold an Aviation Security Identification Card. Be willing and able to pass drug and alcohol testing. Hold a current passport with unrestricted access to all countries Qantas operates to, with at least 12 months validity. Qantas is an equal opportunity employer, so by coming to work for us, you’ll be part of an organisation that encourages diversity, supports charities and environmental initiatives. We encourage Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander, and people of all backgrounds to apply. Be part of something special and play your part in the Qantas story - get in touch today.
