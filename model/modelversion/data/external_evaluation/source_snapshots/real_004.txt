Software Engineer (Full Stack - React/C#). Employer: ITSM. About the Role We are looking for an experienced Junior to Intermediate Developer to join our team at our Sydney Head Office. You won’t just be building simple UI components; you’ll be responsible for crafting high-performance client-side experiences that handle complex, large-scale data sets. While this role is "client-strong," we utilize C# and .NET for our backend services. We need someone who understands how data flows from the database to the user’s screen and can ensure that journey is seamless, efficient, and performant. The Team You will be joining a high-performing engineering team that operates across a hybrid model. This role is specifically based in our Sydney Head Office, the heart of our business operations with over 500 staff on-site. We value the collaboration and professional connection that comes from being part of our HQ. This role operates on a structured hybrid model, requiring four days per week in the Sydney office with one day working from home. You will have the opportunity to work closely with local business stakeholders while collaborating daily with the wider engineering team. Our Perspective on AI We embrace the future of development. We expect our engineers to be AI-literate , leveraging tools like GitHub Copilot and other AI assistants to boost productivity and automate repetitive tasks. However, we value critical thinking above all else; you must have the foundational knowledge to audit, refine, and take full ownership of the code you ship. Requirements Must Have Experience: 2–4 years of professional software development experience. Education: Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent industry experience. Client-Side: Solid proficiency in React and TypeScript . Backend Core: Practical experience with C# and ASP.NET Core Web API . Database Fundamentals: Foundational SQL knowledge and an understanding of how to work with relational data. Performance: A passion for writing clean, optimized, and pragmatic code that handles large data sets efficiently. Modern Web: Solid understanding of Responsive Web Design across all device types. Version Control & DevOps: Experience with Git and familiarity with DevOps CI/CD pipelines . Rights: Full Australian working rights. Nice to Have Styling: Proficiency with Tailwind CSS . SQL Server: Experience writing and maintaining Stored Procedures . Data Mapping: Familiarity with Dapper . Testing: Experience with E2E and unit testing tools like Playwright, Vitest, or React Testing Library . Architecture: Understanding of SOLID principles or common design patterns. State Management: Knowledge of patterns/libraries for managing complex state (e.g., Zustand, Redux Toolkit ). Tooling: Exposure to modern build tools like Vite and API documentation (e.g., Swagger ). What You'll Do Full Stack Contribution: Take ownership of features end-to-end, from API design through to the final React component. Problem Solving: Translate complex business requirements into robust, effective technical solutions. Performance Engineering: Build responsive UIs that remain snappy and reliable while handling heavy data interactions. HQ Collaboration: Work as a key technical presence in our Sydney office, collaborating with local business units and the wider national engineering team. About Us We are an established digital marketing and services management firm based in Darlinghurst, where talented professionals collaborate to solve complex challenges using cutting-edge technologies. As part of our team, you'll work alongside experienced colleagues in our modern office space with panoramic city views. With a workforce of nearly 500 employees, we offer the stability and resources of a large organization while maintaining the collaborative culture and agility of a boutique firm. We are committed to fostering community engagement through multiple annual company events, including charitable initiatives and volunteer opportunities that make a meaningful impact. This role requires a hybrid work arrangement combining office and remote work.
