Remote Software Engineer / Systems Administrator. Employer: Private Advertiser. About the Role We are seeking a Software Engineer / Systems Administrator to help build, maintain, and deploy our bespoke analytics software that is embedded directly within client environments. This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who enjoys writing high-quality software, takes pride in robust deployment and operation, and is excited to work in a modern, AI-native fashion. You will work closely with our small senior team on a proven product while having genuine influence over how it is engineered and operated. Employment Type: Flexible – Contract, Consulting, or Employee Hours: Flexible – between 10 and 15 hours per week. We ask for a minimum of around 2 hours each working day during business hours to support timely responses to urgent client issues or requests. Beyond that, any hours worked above 10 per week can be completed at whatever times suit you. Rate: $60/hour (inclusive of super if applicable, excluding GST if applicable) Location: Onshore Australia (fully remote). Quarterly in-person workshops will be organised and may require travel to one of the eastern capital cities (Brisbane, Sydney, or Melbourne). Reports to: Managing Partner Clearance: Due to the sensitivity of the data involved, a satisfactory National Police Check is a mandatory requirement of this role, and applicants must be willing to undertake further background checks as required. About Us We're an established company with a startup mindset – we have a proven product and an experienced team, but we're constantly innovating and experimenting with new approaches to better serve our clients. Our software is deployed into sensitive client environments, so we care deeply about engineering quality, security, and reliability. We're a small, collaborative team that values autonomy, curiosity, and people who take ownership of their work. Ideal Candidate You're a capable software engineer who writes clean, maintainable code and cares about doing things properly. You're comfortable working independently in a remote setting but thrive on collaboration. You're pragmatic and adaptable – happy working primarily in Python and SQL, but equally willing to pick up and work with whatever language or tooling a problem calls for. Crucially, you're excited about working in an AI-native fashion: you understand how to use modern coding assistants safely and effectively to rapidly develop and deploy software, without compromising on quality or security. Industry experience isn't required – we value the right attitude, skills, and willingness to learn.
